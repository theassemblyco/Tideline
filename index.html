Tideline

    Find your best window on the water

    Search for a beach, river, boat ramp or town (or paste coordinates from Google Maps). Tideline combines tides, solunar periods, moon phase, weather and swell into an hour-by-hour score for the next 7 days.

    Tip: star a spot to save it, then install the app from your browser menu (“Add to Home screen”).

    Settings

    Weather, swell and tide data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0). Moon calculations by SunCalc.

    Tides are modelled for the nearest open-water grid cell and can be out by an hour or more inside rivers and bays. Not for navigation – always check the official BOM / MSQ tide tables and marine warnings before heading out.
